# Notes on the case study
This case study accompanies you throughout the entire semester. It consists of five chapters, each of which is read before the corresponding touchpoint. You read Chapter A before Touchpoint 1, Chapter B is added before Touchpoint 2, and so on. Each new chapter builds on the previous one and extends the information base you work with.
The case deliberately contains more information than you need for the respective touchpoint. Not all details are equally relevant. The challenge is to identify the decisive aspects and to recognise the less important ones.
| Chapter | Read before | Focus |
| A | Touchpoint 1 | Discipline: Foundations (framework) │ Who is ON? Market environment, challenges, stakeholders |
| B | Touchpoint 2 | Discipline: Strategy │ Strategic crossroads: where should ON grow? |
| C | Touchpoint 3 | Discipline: Market access & marketing │ Pricing decision, channel decision, positioning and their coherence |
| D | Touchpoint 4 | Discipline: SCM / Operations │ Supply chain, make-or-buy, cost structure, sustainability |
| E | Touchpoint 5 | Discipline: Integration │ Business model, design dimensions, reflection |
# Note on working with this case
The following applies to all touchpoint assignments: only the facts, data and representations in this document are authoritative. Information about ON from the internet, from annual reports or from other sources may supplement your argumentation, but does not replace the case information. If internet sources and case data contradict each other, the case prevails. The same principle applies in the exam: there, only the information in the exam case is authoritative, not that of ON. This also protects you from misinformation and ensures that all students work on the same basis.
